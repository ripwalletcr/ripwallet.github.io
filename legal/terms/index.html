01

Description of the service

Tabs is a personal finance tracking tool that helps you record and estimate:

  • Debts (loans, credit cards, etc.) and their projected payoff timeline based on the interest rate, term, and payments you enter
  • Investments, savings, and other assets, along with their return on investment
  • Your overall net worth over time, calculated from the figures you enter

All calculations are performed locally on your device, based entirely on the data you provide.
