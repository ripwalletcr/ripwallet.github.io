Privacy Policy

Thank you for using Tabs (the "App"), an application developed by Joseph Murillo ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy is intended to inform you about the types of information that may be collected when you use the App and how we safeguard your privacy. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Tabs is designed to collect no user data whatsoever. We do not collect any personal information, device data, or any other data from users of the App. All of the information you enter — debts, investments, payment history, and net worth history — is stored only on your device using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. There is no backend server and no account system, and none of your financial data is ever transmitted to us.

2. Permissions

The App may request permission to send you local notifications, used solely to remind you of upcoming debt payments or recurring investment deposits based on the dates and settings you provide. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device — no notification content is sent to us or to any third party. The App may also offer an optional biometric lock (Face ID/Touch ID) to protect access to the App; this authentication is handled entirely by iOS on your device, and we never receive or store any biometric data. You can disable either permission at any time in your device's Settings without affecting the App's core functionality.

3. Currency Exchange Rates

To convert amounts between currencies, the App periodically fetches publicly available exchange rate data from a third-party rate provider (open.er-api.com). This request only retrieves general currency rate information and does not include any of your personal or financial data.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use any cookies, tracking technologies, analytics, or similar methods to track your activities on the App.

5. Data Export and Import

Tabs lets you export your own debt and investment data to a CSV file and import it back in. These files are created and shared directly from your device (for example, via the share sheet) and are never transmitted to us or to any server we operate.

6. In-App Purchases

Tabs offers an optional one-time purchase ("Tabs Pro") processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive or store any payment, billing, or card information — all purchase transactions are handled directly by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices.

7. Third-Party Services

Aside from the currency exchange rate lookup described above, Tabs does not integrate with any third-party services or platforms that would collect user data.

8. Children's Privacy

Tabs is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under the age of 13, please contact us immediately at ripwallet.cr@gmail.com, and we will take steps to delete the information as soon as possible.

9. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement industry-standard security measures to protect any potential data that could be collected. However, since we do not collect any user data, there is no personal or sensitive information to secure on our end — your financial data remains under your control, stored locally on your own device.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The most current version of the Privacy Policy will always be available within the App. Any updates to the Privacy Policy will be effective when posted. Your continued use of the App after the effective date of any changes to the Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of those changes.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact us at ripwallet.cr@gmail.com. By downloading and using Tabs, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to be bound by its terms. Thank you for trusting us with your privacy, and we hope you enjoy using Tabs!
